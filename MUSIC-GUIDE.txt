MUSIC ON STREAM  -  licensed music, quieter while your chat is read aloud

WHAT IT DOES
Plays songs from a folder on your PC (mp3, wav, ogg, flac, m4a, aac, wma, opus), with play, pause, next, previous and shuffle.
While ChatVoice reads a chat message aloud the music drops to a lower level (you choose how low), then comes back.
A "now playing" box can appear on stream (OBS Browser source from the Music page).
The Soundboard imports local audio effects and plays them independently over the background music. You can select an imported sound
as the OBS alert notification sound from the OBS overlays page.

STREAMBEATS DOWNLOADS IN CHATVOICE
1. Choose "Browse StreamBeats catalog" to browse the official catalog inside ChatVoice. Preview tracks on the site and
   download eligible audio/album ZIPs; supported downloads are imported into the ChatVoice music library automatically.
2. The "Download free music" button remains available to open StreamBeats' official Bandcamp page in your browser.
   You can manually import those downloads with "Import downloaded tracks / album ZIP".
3. Imported tracks are copied into %APPDATA%\ChatVoice\Music and added to the playlist. ZIP imports only copy supported
   audio formats; unsafe archive paths are ignored. Double-click an imported track to play it, or keep using a local folder.

SOUNDBOARD
1. Choose "Browse free sound effects" to open the Mixkit catalog inside ChatVoice. Preview the available effects on the site,
   then download eligible files; audio files and ZIPs are added to the soundboard automatically.
2. You can also choose "Import sounds / ZIP" to add audio files or an archive from local storage.
3. Double-click a sound or choose Play selected. Effects play over background music. Use OBS overlays > Alert sound file
   to choose an imported effect for on-screen alerts.
Imported effects are stored in %APPDATA%\ChatVoice\Soundboard.
Check Mixkit's current sound-effects license and each provider's terms before use. ChatVoice does not bundle or redistribute
third-party sound effects.

STREAMBEATS LICENSE
StreamBeats' published license says its tracks may be synchronized with Twitch and YouTube videos, worldwide,
royalty-free, perpetual, and without required attribution. Read the current terms before use:
https://www.streambeats.com/licensing
The license permits stream use but cannot guarantee that an automated copyright/DMCA system will never mistakenly
flag a stream. Keep the official license page and your download details as evidence if a claim needs a dispute.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
It does not bundle or redistribute provider music. The download is completed from the provider; ChatVoice imports your
own downloaded tracks and does not independently verify their license.

WHERE TO GET OTHER MUSIC (read each exact license)
- YouTube Audio Library  YouTube describes its tracks as copyright-safe on YouTube; that does not prove Twitch rights
- Incompetech            free tracks may require artist credit
- Mixkit / Free Music Archive  individual terms vary; check permission for each platform
Name files like "Artist - Title.mp3" so they show nicely on screen.

STAY SAFE
- "No copyright" does not mean a claim can never happen. Keep the license page and download proof for every track.
- Credit the artist whenever the license asks. The now-playing box shows title and artist on screen.
- Never play songs from Spotify, YouTube Music, radio or other artists unless you have written permission.

OBS AUDIO
Music and the chat voice both play from ChatVoice, so OBS "Application Audio Capture" picks up both together.
If you want separate faders, play music in an OBS Media Source instead and leave this page for the now-playing box only.
