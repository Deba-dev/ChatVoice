MUSIC ON STREAM  -  no-copyright music, quieter while your chat is read aloud

WHAT IT DOES
Plays songs from a folder on your PC (mp3, wav, ogg, flac, m4a, aac, wma, opus), with play, pause, next, previous and shuffle.
While ChatVoice reads a chat message aloud the music drops to a lower level (you choose how low), then comes back.
A "now playing" box can appear on stream (OBS Browser source from the Music page).

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
It does not download music, and it does not check licences. It plays only the files you give it. You decide what is allowed.

WHERE TO GET MUSIC YOU CAN USE  (always open the licence of the track you pick)
- Pixabay Music        free to use, credit usually not required
- YouTube Audio Library  free tracks; some require credit
- StreamBeats          music made for streamers
- Incompetech          free, but you MUST credit the artist
- Mixkit               free music under its own licence
- Free Music Archive   many different licences, read each one
Name files like  "Artist - Title.mp3"  so they show nicely on screen.

STAY SAFE
- "No copyright" tracks are sometimes flagged by mistake. Keep the licence page of every track you use.
- Credit the artist whenever the licence asks. The now-playing box shows title and artist on screen.
- Never play songs from Spotify, YouTube Music, radio or other artists without written permission: platforms can mute your
  videos, remove clips or strike your channel.

OBS AUDIO
Music and the chat voice both play from ChatVoice, so OBS "Application Audio Capture" picks up both together.
If you want separate faders, play music in an OBS Media Source instead and leave this page for the now-playing box only.
