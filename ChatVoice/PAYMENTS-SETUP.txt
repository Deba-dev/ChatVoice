PAYMENTS  -  viewers pay on the streamer's own Razorpay page, ChatVoice reads name, amount and message aloud
Money goes straight from the viewer to the streamer's Razorpay account. Neither ChatVoice nor the cloud ever holds it.

DO FIRST: the Discord page (server key). Payments use the same connection. (The cloud needs the D1 step in CLOUD-SETUP.)

1. In Razorpay, make a Payment Page. Add two fields and label them exactly  Name  and  Message.
   ChatVoice finds them by their labels (anything containing "name" and "message" works).
2. In ChatVoice > Payments: type a secret you choose (any long random text), click "Save secret".
   A webhook address appears. Click Copy.
3. In Razorpay > Settings > Webhooks > Add: paste the address, paste the SAME secret, tick ONLY the event
   "payment.captured". Save.
4. Use Razorpay TEST MODE first. Make a test payment on your page. ChatVoice should say the name, amount and message.
5. In ChatVoice tick "Listen for payments and read them aloud".

HOW IT BEHAVES
- "Send a test payment" in the app tests the voice only (it does not go through Razorpay).
- A message with a blocked word is never read; the name and amount still are.
- Under the minimum amount (INR only) the message is not read; name and amount still are.
- The same payment is never read twice (Razorpay retries are ignored).
- Alerts normally arrive within seconds.

HONEST LIMITS
- I confirmed the signature check and the payment.captured event in Razorpay's documentation. I have NOT seen a real
  payment from your page: where Razorpay puts the custom fields can differ by page type. If the name or message
  does not show up in test mode, send me the webhook payload from the Razorpay dashboard and I will adjust it.
- International cards must be enabled by Razorpay on the streamer's account; they may ask for documents.
- Razorpay is the only gateway built in. Tell me if you want another one.
